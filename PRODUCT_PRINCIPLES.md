# Mihrab Product Principles

1. Mihrab helps me act, not manage my life.
2. The plan is fixed; execution adapts.
3. Missed work never becomes debt.
4. Essentials come before optimization.
5. No feature without a clear job.
6. Quiet by default; live effects yield to focus.
7. Offline first and private by default.
8. Show less when more is not useful.
9. Content can evolve without code changes.
10. Enough is enough.
